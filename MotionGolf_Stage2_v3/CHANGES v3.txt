Motion Golf - Stage 2 v3

Changes requested:

1. Difficulty presets
Easy:
- Lower tilt sensitivity
- Lower maximum speed
- Stronger natural grass slowdown
- Higher jump power
- Higher allowed hole-entry speed
- Sand is still a trap, but slightly less severe

Normal:
- Balanced values

Hard:
- Higher tilt sensitivity
- Higher maximum speed
- Ball keeps momentum longer on grass
- Lower jump power
- Lower allowed hole-entry speed
- Sand trap is most severe

2. Precise hole entry
The old rule allowed the ball to complete the level when its center was close to
the hole (distance < hole radius + 4). That could count edge contact.

The new rule requires the ENTIRE ball to fit inside the hole:
distance(ball center, hole center) + ball radius <= hole radius

The ball must also be slow enough. When valid, the ball is locked to the hole,
shrinks for 0.45 seconds, and only then completes the level.

3. Strong sand trap
While the ball is grounded in sand:
- tilt input is reduced to a tiny percentage
- speed is heavily damped
- the ball is effectively almost stuck

When the player jumps:
- sand resistance is removed while airborne
- normal tilt acceleration works again
- the player can tilt + jump to escape the sand

A "SAND TRAP - JUMP TO ESCAPE" hint appears while trapped.
